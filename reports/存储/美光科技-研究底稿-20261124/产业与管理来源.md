## 1. Primary annual filing: SCA, cycle mechanics, concentration

Read: [FY26 Form 10-K, filed October 9, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/723125/000072312526000023/mu-20260903.htm).

- Fiscal year ended September 3. FY26 DRAM ASP approximately +180%, NAND ASP approximately +200%; each bit shipment growth mid-20% range. Conventional-memory pricing explains much of extraordinary profitability; HBM is not the sole engine.
- SCA: multi-year take-or-pay specific enforceable volumes; majority pricing subject to floor/ceiling. Management says floors should yield margins above previous peak quarterly margins; this is a forecast, not independently guaranteed economics. At September 3, RPO about $134bn; about one-quarter next 12 months, remaining within 5 years.
- Received $12.747bn cash deposits, nearly all refundable in 2029–31 if customers meet commitments. **Correction: deposits are financing cash flow, not CFO. Do not subtract from CFO/FCF again.** Can reserve return liability when assessing distributable net cash.
- No customer >=10% of FY26 revenue; one customer 17% FY25. Some equipment/inputs single sourced. Taiwan fab network, export controls, China CAC restriction and fixed-cost utilization remain tail risks.

## 2. September 30 update: distinguish snapshot dates

Read: [Q4 prepared remarks, September 30, 2026](https://s25.q4cdn.com/621799436/files/doc_financials/2026/q4/Q4-FY26-Prepared-Remarks.pdf), pp.2–6,9.

- 26 SCAs estimated >35% revenue through 2030; three-quarters of estimated SCA revenue has defined pricing framework, remainder periodically market negotiated. Financial commitments $32bn, vast majority cash deposits; RPO updated about $150bn. Do not mix with September 3 balance-sheet cash/deposits.
- Vast majority CY27 HBM bits contracted with significant year-on-year price increases; HBM4 ramp and NVIDIA custom HBM4E “NVHBM” collaboration announced. Crucial analytical clue: higher HBM mix offset pricing gains in cloud unit margin, so conventional DRAM currently has richer margin; “HBM=always highest margin” is false.
- Management predicts NAND and DRAM still constrained 2027–28; it admits PC/mobile units could fall double digits. Keep this forecast explicitly separate from newer independent NAND outlook below.

Read separately: [Q4 earnings deck](https://s25.q4cdn.com/621799436/files/doc_financials/2026/q4/Q4-26-Earnings-Deck.pdf).

- Global production timetable: ID1 first wafer mid-CY27; ID2 late-CY28; NY first wafer CY30; Tongluo meaningful shipment mid-CY27; Singapore HBM packaging early-CY27 and NAND fab second-half CY28; Japan expansion late-CY28. First wafer is not immediate full output.
- FY27 capex guide: Q1 ~$11.5bn, first half ~$25bn, second half higher; net of expected government incentives. These are capex commitments, not instant bit-capacity growth.

## 4. Strongest recent bear evidence

Read: [TrendForce November 19 NAND outlook](https://www.trendforce.com/presscenter/news/20261119-13314.html).

Forecast CY27 industry NAND bit supply+25–30%, capex+26.7% to$29.9bn. Chinese CSP cuts exceed North American CSP demand additions; GPU constraints, inventory and token-price competition cited. NAND spot prices recently corrected >20%; CY1Q27 contract price0% to-5%, all categories price downcycle from2Q. This directly conflicts with September30 Micron NAND-supply-tight forecast. It does not by itself establish HBM oversupply. Watch contract-price actuals and customer inventories instead of pretending either forecast is settled fact.

Read: [TrendForce November11 PC inventory](https://www.trendforce.com/presscenter/news/20261111-13303.html).

Notebook shipmentCY3Q26 QoQ-15.8%; earlier memory price spikes encouraged over-ordering. Finished-goods and OEM memory inventories building; lower specifications and reduced client-SSD procurement planned. CY4Q PC DRAM still+13–18%, clientSSD<+5%: demand destruction and rising prices can coexist for a while.

Read: [TrendForce November6 smartphone reballed DRAM](https://www.trendforce.com/presscenter/news/20261106-13297.html).

Reballed-memory smartphone penetration estimated3–4% CY26 and7–9% CY27, concentrated budget segment. This is evidence of affordability constraints, not proof DRAM price collapse; TrendForce itself expects little disruption to upward DRAM pricing from this small bit pool.

## 5. TAM / technological moat

Read: [Micron Q1 FY26 earnings deck, December17 2025](https://investors.micron.com/static-files/530bd7ed-a8c8-4687-af4a-8c129f740e09).

Management then forecast HBM TAM about$35bnCY25 to$100bnCY28 (~40%CAGR), pulled forward2years. **This is a dated management forecast, not independently verified latest TAM.** SeptemberQ4 documents reviewed do not repeat a revised TAM number. Do not cite reddit claims of$100bnCY27 without primary call evidence.

Assessment (inference): MU scale/process/yield/customer qualification/advanced packaging are material barriers; few firms can fund a leading memory fab and qualify into HBM platforms. But DRAM bits remain substitutable once qualified, pricing still supply-driven, no network effect, and process leadership must be continuously repurchased through capex/R&D. SCA co-design increases switching friction and visibility for covered programs; it does not lock every revenue dollar forever. HBM4/base-die customization, 3D DRAM, packaging yields and competing architectures are ongoing route risks. A sample or benchmark claim is not equivalent to mass-production economics.
